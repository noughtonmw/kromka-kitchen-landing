# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: React with lightweight CSS and browser-native interactions; no heavy UI framework so the landing page stays fast on mobile.

## Users

Primary users are small-business owners and private specialists who need a conversion-focused one-page website for paid traffic. They are comparing agencies and freelancers, want clarity on the result and budget, and are short on time and marketing expertise.

## Product Purpose

This is a demonstrative portfolio landing page for a digital marketer who creates selling landing pages and can connect ad traffic and analytics. Success is a completed lead-magnet quiz, not a hard sale.

## Positioning

The portfolio demonstrates a complete acquisition path in one place: a landing page built around a real visitor decision, a short quiz, a clear calculation, and measurement-ready thinking.

## Operating Context

The page is shown as a portfolio case and should also be credible as a live concept for a local-service business. Visitors arrive from a mobile device or desktop, scan quickly, compare trust signals, and decide whether to request a free audit/calculation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One primary conversion action: start and complete a short quiz.
- All CTA buttons open the same quiz flow.
- The quiz ends with contact capture and an illustrative result/bonus.
- Responsive from small mobile screens through wide desktop.
- Motion is purposeful and light; content remains usable with reduced motion.
- Claims and proof must be presented as a concept/demo unless supplied as real evidence.

## Brand Commitments

The user asked for a beautiful, layered, niche-specific landing page that feels authored rather than AI-generated, with clear copy and a single CTA. No existing brand assets were supplied.

## Evidence on Hand

The user has real portfolio cases in stretch-ceiling installation, air-conditioning installation, massage services, and emergency locksmithing with Yandex Direct and Yandex Metrica. No client logos, photos, testimonials, or verified performance numbers were supplied for this demo.

## Product Principles

1. Show the acquisition logic, not just a pretty page.
2. Make the next step smaller than a sale.
3. Keep every section answerable in seconds.
4. Make illustrative proof explicit rather than pretending it is live evidence.
5. Optimize for fast comprehension and fast mobile interaction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, keyboard-focusable controls, visible focus states, sufficient color contrast, explicit labels, and a reduced-motion mode. Avoid relying on color alone for meaning.
