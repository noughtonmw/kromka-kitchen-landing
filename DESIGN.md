# Design system — Кромка

## World

The page belongs to the world of a small architectural kitchen studio and a physical material library: draft sheets, cabinet samples, practical marks, and confident editorial typography. The material is tactile and warm, but the information stays precise. The visual metaphor is an edge/kromka: the detail that turns a flat board into something finished.

## Surface

Persuade. The visitor should understand the offer and start one lightweight quiz within seconds. The first viewport shows a finished kitchen as the expected result, with offer tags and a concrete discount. The gallery lets visitors explore three real-looking kitchen scenarios without leaving the conversion path.

## Palette

- Paper `#f4efe7` — warm surface and default canvas.
- Paper deep `#e8dfd3` — quieter document layers.
- Ink `#202225` — primary copy and linework.
- Clay `#bc563c` / dark `#9a3f2e` — action and confident editorial emphasis.
- Moss `#b6c55d` — practical optimism and secondary highlight.
- Night `#232b2b` — dense contrast section.

## Type

Display: Bricolage Grotesque, compact and slightly idiosyncratic. Body: DM Sans, calm and readable. Display tracking is tight but not below `-0.065em` for large headings; body copy is kept to readable measures.

## Composition

Asymmetric editorial columns, deliberate paper slabs, one rotated material board, thin drafting rules, solid color fields, and authored geometric diagrams. Elevation is sparse: one soft hero shadow and a few clear offset shadows on physical paper objects.

## Interaction

Every primary CTA opens the same five-step quiz. The quiz is a lightweight dialog with one question per view, instant answer progression, a clear progress bar, and a final contact form. The result gallery switches between three kitchen scenarios with pricing and audience context. FAQ uses disclosure. Motion is limited to purposeful first-load reveal, photo hover scale, gallery state changes, process-row hover, quiz progress, and an optional backdrop blur; reduced motion disables transitions.

## Content truth

The page is written as a live kitchen studio project. Pricing, guarantees, delivery times, and discount are representative commercial claims that must be replaced with the real business terms before launch. No fake client logos, names, testimonials, or third-party proof are used. Generated interior photos are stored in `assets/` and carry embedded generation provenance.
